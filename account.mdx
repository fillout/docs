## Edit account settings

On your dashboard, click your `Account name` followed by `Settings` in the dropdown menu.

![Screenshot 2025-10-09 at 4.20.42 PM copy.png](/images/Screenshot2025-10-09at4.20.42PMcopy.png)

Here, you'll see your team members, invites, account plan, and more.

## Change account email

If you want to change the email linked to your account, go to `My account` and update your email.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-12-11at5.11.47PM.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2025-12-11 at 5.11.47 PM.png"
  title="Screenshot 2025-12-11 at 5.11.47 PM.png"
  style={{ width:"86%" }}
/>

<Warning>
  Changing your email address in account settings only applies to your currently selected organization. To update your email for other organizations, you must switch to each one and change it manually.
</Warning>
