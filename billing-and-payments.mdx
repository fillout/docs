## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

You can directly access billing [here](https://app.fillout.com/home/settings/billing).

![Screenshot 2025-11-14 at 6.08.04 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-11-14at6.08.04PM.png)

Alternatively, click your `Account name` on the upper left corner followed by `Settings`. Then, click `Billing & Usage` in left the side to `Browse plans`.

<Check>
  Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing cycle, and you won’t be charged afterward unless you upgrade again. Enterprise customers should contact their account manager or email [support@fillout.com](mailto:support@fillout.com)
</Check>

### Payment methods

**Starter, Pro, Business, and Team:** Credit card only

**Enterprise:** In addition to credit card payments, Enterprise customers can pay via wire transfers, purchase orders (POs), invoice payments, and ACH.

<Info>
  If you are interested in an Enterprise plan, please reach out to our team [here](https://www.fillout.com/enterprise).
</Info>

## Get invoices

You can access prior billing invoices in the [billing portal](https://app.fillout.com/home/settings/billing). Click on an invoice to download it.
