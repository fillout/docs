## Overview

You can update your Zite database indirectly using forms, or by allowing others to submit data. This ensures your database stays accurate and up-to-date without giving direct access to your tables, reducing errors and maintaining security.

## Automatic field mapping

If you’ve already created columns in your Zite database and fields in Fillout forms before connecting them, Zite will automatically map matching fields (e.g., Name → Name, Contact Number → Phone Number).

![Screenshot 2026 03 27 At 6 22 10 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-27at6.22.10PM.png)

If not, Zite will pull in any columns or fields from your database and turn them into form fields. These **connected fields appear in the left-hand panel**, ready to be dragged and dropped into your form.

![Screenshot 2026 03 27 At 6 24 09 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-27at6.24.09PM.png)

<Warning>
  Login page fields (like email) aren’t automatically linked and must be mapped manually.
</Warning>

## Map fields manually

To map fields or columns added after the connection was made, follow the steps [here](https://www.fillout.com/help/additional-integration-mappings#how-to-map-fields-manually). You can even [change existing mapping and update options](https://www.fillout.com/help/additional-integration-mappings#updating-fields-and-options).
