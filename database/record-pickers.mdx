## How it works

You can add a dropdown to your Fillout form that lets respondents select existing records from your Zite database. The record picker **only links to the selected record**. It does not update existing records.

If you want form respondents to edit records in your database without giving them direct access, see [this article](https://www.fillout.com/help/database/update-with-forms).

### Common use cases

- **Employee Onboarding:** Let HR select a manager, department, or mentor
- **Equipment Checkout:** Allow staff to select equipment or devices from a database
- **Course Enrollment:** Let students pick existing courses or programs in an enrollment form
- **Vendor Requests:** Link purchase requests to existing suppliers or vendors
- **Content Management:** Let authors select an existing category, project, or campaign

## Add a record picker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link records">
    On your Zite database, link tables by following these quick steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your form">
    Open your Fillout form and navigate to `Integrate` on top and choose `Zite Database`. Then, set your preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a record picker">
    If you have Zite [automatically maps fields](https://www.fillout.com/help/database/map-fields#automatic-column-or-field-mapping) if they are present in both the form and database. In any case, you can still [manually map](https://www.fillout.com/help/additional-integration-mappings#how-to-map-fields-manually) them.

    If you haven't created a field yet to connect the records to, Zite column fields will automatically appear on the left-hand panel. Drag the `record picker` to be used to have respondents select onto your form. This is now your record picker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Click `Publish` on the top right to reflect the changes.

    That's it! Respondends can now select records from your database. 
  </Step>
</Steps>
