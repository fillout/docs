## How it works

Organization admins can choose a default theme that will automatically be applied to all new forms created in their organization. This helps keep your forms consistent without needing to set a theme every time.

<Info>
  This feature is available on the **Team** and **Enterprise** plans. Click [here](https://www.zite.com/pricing) to learn more.
</Info>

## How to set a default theme

From the dashboard, click your `Account name` on the top left followed by `Settings`. You'll be redirected to [General](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/general) settings.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 09 01 At 3 51 25 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-09-01-at-3.51.25-PM.png "Screenshot 2026 09 01 At 3 51 25 PM")
</Frame>

Scroll down to toggle `Default theme for new forms`. Then, choose a form theme.
