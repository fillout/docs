## How to add a signature field

In the left-hand panel, look for the `Signature` field. Then, key in your question or statement.

![Screenshot 2025-10-02 at 6.46.51 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-10-02at6.46.51PM.png)

`Publish` your form and share the link to your respondents.

<Info>
  The signature field is available to **Starter**, **Pro**, **Business**, and **Enterprise** users. To learn more about our pricing plans, click [**here**](https://www.fillout.com/pricing).
</Info>

When respondents click on the `Add signature` button, they'll be prompted to sign in a pop-up window. They can **draw**, **type**, or **upload** an image of their signature.

<img
  src="/images/guides/image-24.png"
  alt="PDF Viewer"
  style={{ width:"67%" }}
/>

After confirming a signature, respondents can still click **x** to remove the provided signature.

If you want to disable the type and upload options, click the <Icon icon="gear-complex" iconType="solid" />. In the right-hand panel, turn off the `Show upload option` and `Show type option`.

## Common signature workflows

**Validate authenticity of a submission**

Request e-signatures for your online waivers, non-disclosure agreements, record verification forms, or any workflow that requires verifying the integrity of user-provided data. The signature field is often used to allow respondents to consent and attest to their submission.

**Streamline business processes**

Increasingly, businesses are using e-signatures to approve employee documents, validate transactions, and kick off employee or vendor onboarding. One way to do this is in Fillout is to include relevant documents in your form with the [PDF Viewer](/embed-pdfs) field type. This way your respondents can view documents without leaving the form and add their signature with the signature field type.
