## What is Zite Database?

[Zite Database](https://www.zite.com/database) is a no-code database that combines the flexibility of a database with the familiarity of a spreadsheet. It lets you store, organize, and manage data from your forms and other tools without writing code. By connecting Fillout to Zite Database, you can:

- Organize submissions across multiple tables
- Combine responses from multiple forms into one centralized database
- Create different views of your data without changing the original records
- Pre-fill forms with existing database records to save time and improve data accuracy
- Let respondents select from database records similar to a dynamic dropdown
- Automatically calculate totals, scores, and other values using database formulas
- Use your database as a lightweight CRM, project tracker, client portal, and more

#### What syncing supports

When your Fillout form is connected to a Zite Database, you can:

- Create new records from form submissions
- [Update existing records](https://www.fillout.com/help/guides/update-with-forms) with form data
- [Pre-fetch](https://www.fillout.com/help/guides/pre-fetch-data) existing data into forms
- [Use login forms](https://www.fillout.com/help/guides/use-login-forms) to sync data

    If you choose an **existing database**, you'll have the option to select a **table** as well.

    <img

      title="Screenshot 2026 03 03 At 6 06 54 PM"

  </Step>

### Attachment storage limits

Zite Database's storage limits may vary according to your plan. Total attachment storage refers to the cumulative amount across all databases within an organization.

|  | **Free** | **Pro** | **Business** | **Team** | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max file size per upload | Up to 20 MB | Up to 20 MB | Up to 20 MB | Up to 20 MB | Up to 20 MB |
| Total attachment storage | 1 GB | 10 GB | 100 GB | 100 GB | 1 TB\+ |

## What happens next

Once linked, every form submission creates a new record in your database. You can:

- View and edit responses directly in the database
- Add internal fields that aren't collected through your form
- Enrich database records with custom fields, internal notes, and statuses
- [Build apps](https://www.fillout.com/help/database/build-apps#create-an-app) on top of your form data
- Use the REST API to access your responses programmatically

<Check>
  Form responses sync instantly to your database. Any changes you make in the database won't affect the original form submission.
</Check>

## Common use cases

By connecting Fillout with Zite Database, you can easily build workflows for things like:

- **CRM Systems:** Manage leads, contacts, and customer interactions in one place
- **Project Management:** Track tasks, progress, and deadlines across teams
- **Event Registration:** Collect, organize, and update attendee information in a database
- **Inventory Tracking:** Monitor stock levels, updates, and product movements in real time
- **Client Portals:** Let users view and update their own records securely through forms
- **Feedback Collection:** Centralize responses and analyze them using views and formulas
