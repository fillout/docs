## How it works

Fillout gives you two ways to control the language of your form. You can change the [**Default language**](/make-a-form-in-any-language#how-to-change-form-language) to set the language used for system messages throughout the form, or enable [**translations**](/make-a-form-in-any-language#translate-your-form) to let respondents access the same form in multiple languages. 

If respondents have enabled Google Translate, you can also [disable this automatic translation](/make-a-form-in-any-language#disable-google-translate) from the same language settings.

## How to change form language

Open your form settings by clicking <Icon icon="gear-complex" /> on the upper right area.

![Screenshot 2025-06-26 at 7.41.38 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-06-26at7.41.38PM.png)

Then, click `Language` in the left-hand panel to select your preferred **Default language**. That's it! `Publish` your form and share the link.

![Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 6.24.40 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-10-16at6.24.40PM.png)

Changing the form’s language will affect system messages throughout the form. For example, the text inside this file uploader field type will automatically be translated into Korean.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-10-16at6.07.23PM.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 6.07.23 PM.png"
  title="Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 6.07.23 PM.png"
  style={{ width:"75%" }}
/>

<Warning>
  This feature only translates text that you can't manually edit. Questions, button labels, text, and choice labels will remain the same. However, you are free to edit them.
</Warning>

## Translate your form

Unlike changing your form’s [**Default language**](https://www.notion.so/Create-multilingual-forms-d67d7d8e65f54e76b9c50dd48816f742?pvs=21), which switches it to a single language, translating your form lets respondents access it in multiple languages. Go to your form’s `Language` settings.

![Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 6.26.26 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-10-16at6.26.26PM.png)

Then `Enable translations` and `+ Add` your preferred languages.

## Disable Google Translate

Many Chrome respondents have Google Translate on, which auto-translates websites. If you’ve already set your form to your preferred language, you can stop the respondent’s browser from auto-translating the form.

![Screenshot 2025-10-16 at 6.29.03 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-10-16at6.29.03PM.png)

In your form’s `Language` settings, `Disable auto-translate`.
