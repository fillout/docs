## View and edit existing connections

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Integrations">
    From the dashboard, click your `Account name` followed by `Integrations` in the dropdown menu.

    ![Screenshot2026 02 20at4 33 06PM 3](/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at4.33.06PM-3.png)

    [Here](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/integrations), you’ll see a full list of all apps and services connected to your forms, apps, and databases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View connection details">
    Click any **connection** to see its name, the user who connected it, its status, and which forms and/or apps it’s used in.

    ![Screenshot2026 02 20at4 39 16PM](/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at4.39.16PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit connection">
    If you have multiple connections for the same integration, it helps to `Rename` them so you can easily tell them apart.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at4.39.16PMcopy.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 20at4 39 16P Mcopy"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 20at4 39 16P Mcopy"
      style={{ width:"46%" }}
    />

    You can also `Reconnect` and `Delete` here.

    <Warning>
      Deleting a connection will stop responses from syncing with your integration.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limit workspace connections

You can restrict connections to certain workspaces only. This is helpful if you have clients, partners, or team members with [granular permissions](/granular-permissions) in a specific workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View connection's workspaces">
    On your chosen connection's row, click `All`.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at6.13.42PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 20at6 13 42PM"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 20at6 13 42PM"
      style={{ width:"80%" }}
    />

    <Note>
      Zapier and Make integrations don’t support granular permissions. A workaround is to use the per-form [webhook integration](/webhook) to trigger external automations.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add workspace(s)">
    In the popup, click `Limited workspaces`, then select a **workspace** from the dropdown. 

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at4.51.04PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 20at4 51 04PM"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 20at4 51 04PM"
      style={{ width:"55%" }}
    />

    You can add more than one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click `Save`
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disconnect 3rd party apps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to integrations">
    From the dashboard, click your `Account name` followed by `Integrations` in the dropdown menu.

    ![Screenshot2026 02 20at4 33 06PM 3](/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at4.33.06PM-3.png)

    <Note>
      Only admins can connect 3rd party apps to Fillout. Once connected, make sure only authorized users who should have permission to add webhooks to **all forms across all workspaces** have access.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View apps">
    Go to `Apps`. 

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at5.17.50PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 20at5 17 50PM"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 20at5 17 50PM"
      style={{ width:"77%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an app">
    Hover over the app and click `Disconnect`.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-20at6.03.56PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 20at6 03 56PM"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 20at6 03 56PM"
      style={{ width:"77%" }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>
