## How it works

Generating **QR codes** for forms enables quick and easy mobile access to forms, surveys, or other response materials. This feature enhances user engagement and promotes efficient data collection.

## How to share a form QR

After publishing your form, click <Icon icon="qrcode" />. Alternatively, you can click `Share` in the top center or right.

`Copy` or `Download` this to your device.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-12-01at6.56.50PM.png"

<Note>
  The generated QR code is tied to your form’s current custom link or subdomain (not the default Fillout form link). If you change the custom link or subdomain later, any previously generated QR code will no longer redirect to the updated URL.
</Note>
