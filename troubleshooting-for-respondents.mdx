## How to fix common issues

**Form not loading or blank page**

- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Disable browser extensions (especially ad blockers)
- Check internet connection
- Check that the form hasn’t expired or closed

**Submit button not working**

- Ensure all required fields are filled in (look for red validation messages)
- Scroll up to check for missed fields
- Try refreshing and re-filling if the form seems stuck

**File uploads failing**

- Check [file size limits](https://www.fillout.com/help/file-upload#upload-limits)
- Try a different file format
- Ensure a stable internet connection during upload

**Form looks broken or misaligned**

- Try a different browser or device
- Zoom to 100% (Ctrl/Cmd \+ 0)
- Disable browser zoom or accessibility extensions that may interfere

**Payment not going through**

- Check that your card details are correct and up to date
- Make sure your card hasn't expired
- Check that your card has sufficient funds or available credit
- Check that your card isn’t locked or frozen in your bank or wallet app
- Try a different payment method if available

**Missing partial responses or lost progress**

- Make sure you're using the same device you used to start the form
- If the form requires login, use the same email address to access your saved progress
- If you think you may resume answering a form, avoid clearing browser data
