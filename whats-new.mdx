  ## Launch Week Day 4: MCP & Integrations

  Today we're releasing new ways to connect Zite databases to your AI tools and automation platforms.

  <Card title="Follow along with Launch Week" icon="rocket" href="https://jan2026.zite.so/launch-week">
    Check out the full Launch Week page for daily updates and announcements.
  </Card>

  #### 🤖 Model Context Protocol (MCP)

  Connect your AI assistants to Zite using the [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](/database/mcp/mcp-overview), an open standard that lets AI tools interact with your databases.

  <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/iyz7U-BTyTM" title="Zite MCP" frameborder="0" className="w-full aspect-video rounded-xl" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowfullscreen />

  Use natural language to query, create, and update data in Zite directly from tools like Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any MCP-compatible client.

  Example prompts:
  - "What's the average score in the Test Scores database?"
  - "Create a Zite database with countries of the world and their populations"
  - "Mark all overdue tasks as urgent"

  <Card title="Learn more about MCP" icon="robot" href="/database/mcp/mcp-overview">
    See the full documentation for connecting AI tools to Zite.
  </Card>

  #### ⚡ Zapier integration for Zite Database

  The new [Zite Zapier integration](/database/zapier) connects your Zite databases to thousands of other apps. Trigger Zaps when records are created, updated, or deleted — or push data into Zite from any Zapier-connected app.

  **Triggers:** New Record, Updated Record, Deleted Record, New Table, New Field

  **Actions:** Create Record, Update Record, Delete Record, Create Table, Create Field

  **Searches:** Find Record, Find Table, Find Field

  All triggers use webhooks for instant, real-time triggering with no polling delays.

  <Card title="Learn more about Zapier" icon="bolt" href="/database/zapier">
    See the full documentation for the Zite Zapier integration.
  </Card>

  #### 🔗 Relay.app integration for Zite Database

  The [Zite Relay.app integration](/database/relay) brings human-in-the-loop automations to your databases. Add manual approval or review steps to your workflows, and connect Zite to the rest of your stack.

  **Triggers:** New record added, Record changed, Record first meets conditions

  **Actions:** Add record to table, Update record, Create or update record, Find record(s)

  <Card title="Learn more about Relay.app" icon="bolt" href="/database/relay">
    See the full documentation for the Zite Relay.app integration.
  </Card>

</Update>

  #### 🧠 Think first, build second

  [Plan mode](/zite/plan-mode) acts like a Product Manager for your project. Describe what you want to build, and Zite will ask clarifying questions to nail down the details.

  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2026-01-09at4.42.36PM.png"
    alt="Zite asking clarifying multiple-choice questions in Plan mode"
    style={{ width: "55%" }}

  #### ✏️ Edit before you commit

  Once Zite gathers enough context, it generates an editable plan document — covering scope, data, integrations, key assumptions, and what's out of scope. Click anywhere on the plan to tweak the wording or decisions. You can also click **Help me plan** to have Zite ask a few more questions to refine it further.

  <Frame>
    ![Plan mode showing a plan document with scope and details](/images/Screenshot2026-01-09at4.46.04PM-1.png)
  </Frame>

  #### 🚀 Build when you're ready

  Once the plan looks good, click **Build plan**. Zite will switch to [Build mode](/zite/build-mode) and execute exactly what you approved — no surprises.

  **When to use Plan mode:**
  - Starting a new app and want to think through requirements
  - Your request is large or ambiguous
  - You want to align with teammates before building
  - You want to avoid rework by deciding key details up front

  Switch to Plan mode by clicking the **Mode** selector and choosing **Plan**, or press `Shift` + `Tab`.
