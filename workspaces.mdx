## Overview

Workspaces are like folders where you can organize your forms, apps, and databases.

There are two types of workspaces: **Classic workspaces**, which are used for Fillout forms and can also contain Zite apps and databases, and **Workspaces**, which are designed for organizing Zite apps and databases.

## Create a workspace

On your **dashboard**, hover and click the `plus icon` <Icon icon="square-plus" /> beside Classic workspaces to add a new one.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-10-09at4.37.21PM.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2025-10-09 at 4.37.21 PM.png"
  title="Screenshot 2025-10-09 at 4.37.21 PM.png"
  style={{ width:"31%" }}
/>

You’ll now be redirected to your new **classic** **workspace**, which can also be accessed on the left-hand panel.

## Add a form to a workspace

Click <Icon icon="square-ellipsis" /> followed by `Move to`. Then, choose the workspace you’d like to add the form to.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-10-09at4.40.39PMcopy.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2025-10-09 at 4.40.39 PM copy.png"
  title="Screenshot 2025-10-09 at 4.40.39 PM copy.png"
  style={{ width:"67%" }}
/>

Alternatively, you can drag and drop a form to your preferred workspace.
