## Overview

When your app is [shared to the web](/zite/access/share-to-web), you can require users to sign in before accessing it. Zite handles authentication for you — no custom login code needed.

## Set up authentication

<Steps>
  <Step title="Share your app to the web">
    If you haven't already, enable  [Share to web](/zite/access/share-to-web).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add login">
    Click `Users` on top followed by `Authentication`. Then, click `Add login`.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-19at7.52.20PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 7 52 20 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 7 52 20 PM"
      style={{ width:"81%" }}
    />

    Alternatively, you can type **"Add user login"** in the chatbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sign-in methods">
    Choose which methods to enable:

    - **Magic link** — users receive a sign-in link via email (valid for 10 minutes)
    - **Google** — users sign in with their Google account
    - **SSO** — Users authenticate through your organization's identity provider. [Learn more](/zite/access/sso).

    Magic link and Google are enabled by default.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-19at7.57.40PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 7 57 40 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 7 57 40 PM"
      style={{ width:"84%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can sign up">
    Scroll down to see the **Who can sign up?** section. Then, select one of the ff:

    - **Invite only** (default) - only users you explicitly add can sign in
    - **Anyone** - anybody who visits your app can create an account
    - **Only allowed domains** - only users from specific email domains (e.g., [example.com](http://example.com)) can sign up

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-19at7.58.44PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 7 58 44 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 7 58 44 PM"
      style={{ width:"87%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and deploy">
    Click `Publish` on the upper right corner to reflect the changes, then [share](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/access/share-to-web) your app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage users

You can also specify a list of people who are allowed to use your Zite app. Click `Users` on the top nav bar followed by `Add users`.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-19at8.04.03PM-1.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 8 04 03 PM"
  title="Screenshot 2026 03 19 At 8 04 03 PM"
  style={{ width:"73%" }}
/>

Then enter the emails of the users you'd like to add in the popup.
