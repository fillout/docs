## Overview

Every Zite app is **internal by default** — only members of your organization can access it. When you're ready, you can [share your app to the web](/zite/access/share-to-web) so people outside your organization can use it too.

**Who can access internal apps**

- **Admins** — always have full access to all apps in the organization
- **Invited collaborators** — organization members who have been specifically invited to the app

## Invite collaborators

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Access settings">
    Click `Share` followed by `People with access`.

    ![Screenshot 2026 02 16 At 3 53 53 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-02-16at3.53.53PM.png)

    Then, `Add collaborators...`.  

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-16at3.34.25PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 02 16 At 3 34 25 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 02 16 At 3 34 25 PM"
      style={{ width:"60%" }}
    />

    Alternatively, you can click <Icon icon="gear" /> (beside Help) followed by `Access` in the popup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up access">
    Enter one or more email addresses in the popup. Then, choose an access level for the collaborators:

    - **Edit** (default) - can edit the app in the editor
    - **Use only** - cannot edit the app

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-16at3.38.32PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 02 16 At 3 38 32 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 02 16 At 3 38 32 PM"
      style={{ width:"60%" }}
    />

    <Note>
      If you invite someone with Edit access to an app that has a connected Zite Database, they will also be invited to the underlying database.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invites">
    Click `Send`. Pending invites expire after 2 weeks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage collaborator access

In the **People with access** section, click the access dropdown next to a user's name to:

- **Change access level** — Switch between Edit and Use only
- **Remove access** — Remove the collaborator (you'll be asked to confirm)
- **Revoke pending invites** — Cancel invites that haven't been accepted
