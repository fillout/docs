## Overview

When you share your Zite app to the web, anyone with the link can access it — even people outside your organization. This is ideal for customer-facing portals, public-facing tools, directories and websites.

You can still require sign-in while still sharing your app to the web, through [Authentication](/zite/access/authentication).

## Enable public sharing

`Share` to view the dropdown for **General Access**. Then, choose `Share to web`.

![Screenshot 2026 04 15 At 5 38 59 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-04-15-at-5.38.59-PM.png)

That's it! `Copy` your app's link or click <Icon icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" /> to open it in a new tab.

Alternatively, you can click <Icon icon="gear" /> (beside Help) followed by `Access` in the popup.

<Info>
  If your app uses authentication, you'll be prompted to choose who can sign up. See [Authentication](/zite/access/authentication) for details.
</Info>

## Share a QR code

In the `Share` menu, click `Advanced` and choose <Icon icon="qrcode" /> `QR code`.
