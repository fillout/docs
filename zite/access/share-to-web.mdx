## Overview

When you share your Zite app to the web, anyone with the link can access it — even people outside your organization. This is ideal for customer-facing portals, public-facing tools, directories and websites.

## Enable sharing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Access settings">
    In the app editor, go to **Settings** > **Access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle on Share to web">
    Enable the **Share to web** toggle.

    If your app uses authentication, you'll be prompted to choose who can sign up. See [Authentication](/zite/access/authentication) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the link">
    Click `Share` in the upper right corner, then `Copy` to copy your app's link. Click <Icon icon="arrow-up-right-from-square" /> to open it in a new tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share a QR code

In the **Share** menu, choose <Icon icon="qrcode" /> `QR code`, then `Copy` or `Download` the generated code.

## Public apps (no login required)

If your app doesn't use authentication, anyone with the link can access it without signing in.

To require sign-in while still sharing your app to the web, see [Authentication](/zite/access/authentication).

## Disable sharing

To make your app internal again, go to **Settings** > **Access** and toggle off **Share to web**. External users and guests will lose access immediately.

<Tip>
  Customize your app's domain and link preview from **Settings** > **Link**. Learn more in [Customize links](/zite/customize-links).
</Tip>
