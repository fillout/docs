## Overview

You can require users to authenticate through your organization's **Single Sign-On** (SSO) identity provider — such as Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace — before accessing your Zite app.

SSO gives you centralized control over who can access your app, and allows you to pull in **custom attributes** like department, role, or cost center from your identity provider.

<Info>
  SSO is available on the **Enterprise Plan**. Learn more [here](https://www.fillout.com/enterprise).
</Info>

## Prerequisites

- Your organization must be on Zite's [Enterprise Plan](https://main.fillout.com/zite-demo)
- Your SSO provider must be [configured with Zite](/saml-sso)

## Enable SSO

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Access settings">
    In the app editor, go to **Settings** > **Access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable SSO">
    Under **Sign-in method**, toggle on **Sign in with SSO**.

    <Frame>
      ![Sign-in methods including SSO](/images/images/sso-new-images/enable-sso-zite.png)
    </Frame>

    <Info>
      When SSO is enabled, Magic Link and Google Sign-In are automatically disabled. Only one authentication method can be active at a time.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who can sign up">
    Under **Who can sign up?**, select one of:

    | Setting | Description |
    |---------|-------------|
    | **Invite only** (default) | Only users you explicitly add can sign in |
    | **Anyone** | Anyone who authenticates via SSO can create an account |
    | **Only allowed domains** | Only users from specific email domains can sign up |
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Custom attributes

When SSO is enabled, you can pull in **custom attributes** from your identity provider — such as department, role, or cost center — and use them in your app.

<Frame>
  ![Custom attributes in Access settings](/images/images/sso-new-images/zite-custom-attributes.png)
</Frame>

### Discover attributes

Custom attributes are automatically discovered from actual SSO logins. At least one user must have signed in to a Zite app via SSO for attributes to appear.

Click the **refresh** button to re-scan for new attributes after more users have signed in.

### Select attributes

Each discovered attribute appears as a checkbox. Toggle on the attributes you want available in your app, then click **Save**.

### Use attributes in your app

Once enabled, custom attributes are available on the authenticated user object. For example, if you enable `department`, your app can access the user's department to personalize content or control feature access.

The Zite AI builder also understands these attributes — you can ask it to build features like *"show different content based on the user's department"*.

<Info>
  SSO attributes (including custom attributes) are refreshed every time a user signs in. If a user's department changes in your identity provider, the updated value will appear the next time they log in to the app.
</Info>

## How SSO login works for end users

1. User visits the published Zite app
2. The login page shows a **Sign in with SSO** button and an email field
3. User enters their work email and clicks **Sign in with SSO**
4. They are redirected to your organization's identity provider (e.g., Okta, Azure AD)
5. After authenticating, they are redirected back to the app and signed in
