## Overview

Building multiple apps and landing pages for the same company or brand requires staying within brand guidelines to ensure a consistent look and feel across everything you create. Without a shared system, it can be difficult to keep colors, fonts, and styling aligned across different projects.

A **brand kit** helps solve this by keeping your key brand elements in one place so you can easily apply them across all apps and pages.

## What is a brand kit?

It's a centralized hub containing the visual styling elements that represent your brand. It contains the following:

- **Logo:** The primary visual symbol used in app headers and nav bars
- **Icon:** A small square logo used as favicon and app icon
- **Font:** The main text style
- **Color Palette:** Primary and secondary colors, including accents if any
- **Guidelines:** A set of rules that define how a brand should look and be presented

## Build your brand kit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how to set up">
    From the dashboard, click <Icon icon="swatchbook" /> followed by `Create brand kit`. Alternatively, you can open the [Branding kit settings](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/branding-kit).

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-5.17.30-PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 5 17 30 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 5 17 30 PM"
      style={{ width:"67%" }}
    />

    In the popup, you can either `Import a website` or `Create manually`. For our example, let's use Coca-Cola's website.

    ![Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 5 48 58 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-5.48.58-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or edit your brand assets">
    Importing will automatically capture a website's brand information and styling.

    ![Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 5 49 45 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-5.49.45-PM.png)

    This is includes the logos, color palette, font,  button, and card styles. You are free to edit these however you see fit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review guidelines">
    Importing also captures brand guidelines, specifically\*\* Visual Style\*\*, **Tone of Voice**, and **Business Context**. You can add more to this or edit them as needed.

    ![Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 5 50 19 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-5.50.19-PM.png)

    Click `Create Brand Kit` once you've reviewed all the details.

    That's it! You’ll now see this when starting a new app in Zite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit an existing brand kit

On the dashboard, click your `Account name` followed by `Settings`. 

Navigate to `Branding kit` and click your **brand** to edit it.
