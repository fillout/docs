## Cancel subscription

Go to your [subscription](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing), then downgrade to a **Free** app plan.

![Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 6 45 13 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at6.45.13PM.png)

<Check>
  Canceling your Zite subscription will not cancel your Fillout subscription.
</Check>

For paid plans, click `Cancel`.

<img
  src="/images/cancel.png"
  alt="Cancel"
  title="Cancel"
  style={{ width:"52%" }}
/>

If you'd like to delete your Fillout account altogether including forms, apps, databases, and responses, check [this guide](https://www.fillout.com/help/delete-user-account).
