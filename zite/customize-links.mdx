## Domain vs subdomain

A **domain** is your main website address, like “example.com.” It’s what people type into their browser to visit your site. You buy and register this from a domain registrar like GoDaddy. The good think is [Zite can also do this for you](/zite/customize-links#purchase-a-domain)!

On the other hand, a **subdomain** is an extension added before your main domain, like “blog.example.com.” It’s a separate section of your website.

<Info>
  Custom domains are available for apps once they are [shared to the web](/zite/access/share-to-web).
</Info>

## Add a domain or subdomain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share menu">
    After publishing your app, a random subdomain is generated. You can see this by clicking `Share` in the upper right corner. To edit this, click `Edit link`.

    ![Screenshot2026 02 26at3 21 52PM](/images/Screenshot2026-02-26at3.21.52PM.png)

    Alternatively, you may click <Icon icon="gear" /> (beside Help) to make changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit custom domain">
    In the popup, you can:

    - **Add your own domain** - `Set up` a domain you already own (e.g., medway.com)
    - **Customize Zite subdomain** - `Manage` the generated random subdomain (e.g., ourservicespage.zite.so)

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-26at3.15.48PMcopy.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 02 26 At 3 15 48 PM Copy"
      title="Screenshot 2026 02 26 At 3 15 48 PM Copy"
      style={{ width:"80%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    In the popup, enter your domain or subdomain, then click `Change link`.

    <Info>
      After adding a domain, a popup will show if your domain has been verified. If not, you'll be required to configure the DNS with your domain provider.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

    Enter your **preferred domain** in the search bar. In the list of available domains, `Buy` one that suits your app best.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customize link preview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open link settings">
    Click <Icon icon="gear" /> (beside Help) on the upper right area of your editor.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-26at3.57.31PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 26at3 57 31PM"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 26at3 57 31PM"
      style={{ width:"45%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit preview info">
    Go to `Branding`. Here, you can add a:

    - **Meta title**
    - **Meta description**
    - **Thumbnail**
    - **Favicon**

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-02-26at4.15.54PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot2026 02 26at4 15 54PM"
      title="Screenshot2026 02 26at4 15 54PM"
      style={{ width:"81%" }}
    />

    <Note>
      Preview images are cached by platforms, so it may take time for the updated thumbnail to show. Test your preview [here](https://www.opengraph.xyz).
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>
