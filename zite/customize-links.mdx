<Info>
  Custom domains are available for apps once they are [shared to the web](/zite/access/share-to-web).
</Info>

## Change subdomain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share menu">
    After publishing your app, a random subdomain is generated. You can see this by clicking `Share` in the upper right corner. To edit this, click `Customize link`.

    ![Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.29.24 PM.png](/images/Screenshot2025-12-16at2.29.24PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Pretty URL">
    This doesn't require any custom domain.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-08-20at5.54.55PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 5.54.55 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 5.54.55 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"56%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your subdomain">
    Enter your preferred subdomain, then `Change link`.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-08-20at5.55.21PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 5.55.21 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 5.55.21 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"57%" }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use your custom domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share menu">
    Click `Share` in the upper right corner followed by `Customize link`.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-12-16at2.32.24PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.32.24 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.32.24 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"31%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Custom domain">
    You have to own the domain in order to make changes.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-08-20at5.54.55PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 5.54.55 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 5.54.55 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"56%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Enter your domain.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-08-20at6.13.32PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 6.13.32 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 6.13.32 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"56%" }}
    />

    A popup will show if your domain has been verified. If not, you'll be required to configure the DNS with your domain provider.

</Steps>

## Customize link preview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open share menu">
    Click `Share` followed by `Link settings`.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-12-16at2.34.21PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.34.21 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.34.21 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"32%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit preview info">
    When your app is published, AI generates a default **Title** and **Description**.  You can edit these here.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-12-16at2.38.05PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.38.05 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-12-16 at 2.38.05 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"70%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Thumbnail (Optional)">
    You can `Upload thumbnail` to customize the default image that appears when your app link is shared.

    <Note>
      Preview images are cached by platforms, so it may take time for the updated thumbnail to show. Test your preview [here](https://www.opengraph.xyz).
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Favicon (Optional)">
    You can also `Upload favicon`, which is the icon that appears in a browser tab like the black image seen below. This is usually a company's logo or icon.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2025-08-20at6.19.30PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 6.19.30 PM.png"
      title="Screenshot 2025-08-20 at 6.19.30 PM.png"
      style={{ width:"52%" }}
    />
  </Step>
</Steps>
