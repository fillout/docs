## Overview

Zite gives you flexibility and control over your app’s design. You can style your app by using AI with a reference image, select a theme, or [start with a template](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/marketplace/clone-templates) and edit from there. You can also make manual tweaks with [selective edits](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/selective-edits) and [apply a brand kit](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/brand-kit).

## Enter a prompt

In the chat box, drag and drop an image or click \+ followed by `Upload an attachment`. In your prompt, say whether you want the full design to match the image or just certain parts like fonts or colors.

## Choose a theme

Click the <Icon icon="swatchbook" iconType="solid" /> `paintbrush icon` on the bottom left, then choose your preferences.

## Use a template

If you prefer to start with a template as a base, visit the [Zite Marketplace](https://zite.com/marketplace) and [clone](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/marketplace/clone-templates) your preferred template.
