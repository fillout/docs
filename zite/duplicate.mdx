## Overview

Duplicating apps is helpful if you'd like to use it as a template for a different app, want to use it for a different client but tweak it, or build a version 2 while retaining the first version of your app.

## Duplicate Zite app

From your dashboard, hover over the app you'd like to duplicate, then click <Icon icon="square-ellipsis" /> followed by `Duplicate`.

That's it! An exact copy will be added to the workspace you're currently in. To move this, simply drag and drop the new copy into your preferred workspace.
