## How it works

Build apps powered by advanced AI capabilities using Claude. This enables your app to generate output, process user inputs, and automate intelligent workflows in real time. By connecting Anthropic, you can:

- Generate AI-powered content and responses inside your app
- Process, analyze, and extract data
- Route and automate tasks and workflows
- Build chat boxes within your app
- Use AI-powered text generation across forms, pages, and app features

## How to integrate Anthropic (Claude) into your app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to Anthropic">
    In the chat box, request your app to be integrated with Anthropic or click the `integrations icon` <Icon icon="rectangle-list" />.

## Common use cases

With an Anthropic connection, you can create apps like:

- **Content Generators:** Help users generate text for emails, posts, and documents
- **Analysis Tools:** Let users extract key insights from documents and summarize them
- **Proposal Evaluators:** Let users review and evaluate proposals with AI assistance
- **Chat Bots:** Build AI assistants that respond to user questions in real time
- **Workflow Automation:** Automate tasks and multi-step workflows like approvals
