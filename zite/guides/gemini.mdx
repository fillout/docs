## How it works

Add AI capabilities for text and image processing with Gemini to your app. This enables it to understand and extract data from user uploads or inputs. By connecting Gemini, you can:

- Process and analyze text and images
- Generate summaries and reports from documents or research
- Classify and categorize images
- Review content for compliance and quality
- Enrich records intelligently

## Common use cases

With a Gemini connection, you can create apps like:

- **Expense Approval Tools:** Let employee submit expenses and admin approve or reject them
- **Visual Data Extractors:** Let users pull information from images like receipts, IDs, etc
- **Document Intelligence Tools:** Let users extract key data from forms and reports
- **Content Review Tools:** Evaluate text and image submissions for quality and compliance
- **Data Enrichment Tools:** Let users enhance records using extracted data from uploads
