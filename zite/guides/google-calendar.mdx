## How it works

Let users schedule calls on your Google Calendar by linking it to your Zite app either as a button or an embed. This is useful for meetings, appointments, and other events.

## How to insert your booking page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect to Google Calendar">
    In the chat box, ask Zite to connect to Google Calendar or click <Icon icon="rectangle-list" />.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at12.47.23PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 12 47 23 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 12 47 23 PM"
      style={{ width:"36%" }}
    />

    Then, sign in to your Google Account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your use case">
    Describe how you'd like to use Google Calendar in your app. In this example, we'll let users book a call after seeing their generated quote.

    ![Screenshot 2026 04 01 At 5 30 27 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-04-01at5.30.27PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert booking link">
    Paste the booking link of your Google Calendar in the chat.

    <img
      src="/images/Screenshot2026-04-01at5.36.03PM.png"
      alt="Screenshot 2026 04 01 At 5 36 03 PM"
      title="Screenshot 2026 04 01 At 5 36 03 PM"
      style={{ width:"39%" }}
    />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and share">
    That's it! Users can now schedule a call with you through your app. Invite confirmations will be sent to the recipient's email along with a meeting link (based on your Google Calendar preferences) and a link to cancel.

    ![Screenshot 2026 04 01 At 5 49 49 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-04-01at5.49.49PM.png)

    Click `Publish` in the upper right corner to [**share**](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/access/share-to-web) or [**embed**](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/embedding) your app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a custom booking page

If you don’t want to use the standard Google Calendar design, you can create a [branded Fillout scheduling form](https://www.fillout.com/help/fillout-scheduling-intro) instead. Then [link the form to your Zite app](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/guides/fillout) so others can book appointments with your custom design.

## Common use cases

With a Google Calendar connection, you create apps like:

- **Product Sites:** Let potential customers book demo calls
- **Team Meetings:** Let team members book meeting slots without back-and-forth emails
- **Bookings or Appointments:** Allow others to book appointments like patients and etc.
- **Interview Scheduling:** Let candidates pick available interview slots
- **Consultations:** Let clients schedule virtual consults
