## How it works

Turn your HubSpot CRM into a fully functional Zite app that users can access without HubSpot, so you don’t need to pay for per-seat licenses.

Once connected, Zite reads and writes HubSpot data in real time, keeping your apps in sync. By connecting Zite to HubSpot, you can:

- Build custom views for contacts, companies, and deals (pipelines, directories, dashboards)
- Let your team update CRM data from Zite, with changes syncing back to HubSpot
- Trigger emails, Slack alerts, or document generation based on CRM events

## Common use cases

By creating a Zite app with HubSpot, you can quickly build tools like:

- **Sales Dashboards:** Visualize contacts, companies, and deals in custom pipeline views
- **Deal Tracking Apps:** Monitor deal stages, updates, and progress in real time
- **Customer Directories:** Build searchable views of contacts and accounts
- **Workflow Apps:** Trigger actions like emails, Slack alerts, or updates based on CRM events
- **Custom CRM Views:** Let teams manage HubSpot data in a cleaner, more usable interface
