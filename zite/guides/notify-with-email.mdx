## Overview

You can automatically send emails and notifications based on your workflows. This is useful for confirmations, reminders, receipts, and more. Four email types are supported:

| **Type** | **Sender** | **Use-case** |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| [Zite](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/guides/notify-with-email#how-to-automate-with-zite-mail) | Built-in email | Internal or testing |
| Gmail | Personal or Google Workspace | Personal or team inboxes |
| Outlook | Personal or Work email | Personal or team inboxes |
| Custom SMTP | Email providers (e.g. SendGrid, Mailgun, Loops) | Scalable, high-volume or production email |

<Note>
  When using Zite, the sender name will appear as “Notifications” in the inbox. When using your own email account, the sender name defaults to your account name.
</Note>

The fastest way to start sending emails from your app is with the **Zite** built-in option. This is intended for testing or internal apps, and emails will be sent from [notify@mail.zite.com](mailto:notify@mail.zite.com)

## Customize your email

Aside from the custom email button that shows up during connection, you can access the email editor from the `Workflows` page (top) by selecting the email workflow. Here, you can:

- Edit the sender email or use a dynamic email field
- Edit the default subject and body
- Change font weight, add bullets, and more by **highlighting text**
  <img
    src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at3.14.18PM.png"
    alt="Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 3 14 18 PM"
    title="Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 3 14 18 PM"
    style={{ width:"69%" }}
  />
- Customize button text, alignment, and URL
- Insert dynamic data like name and preferences (orange tags) to personalize email by **typing @ and selecting a field**

<Info>
  Fields are recognized by Zite as values from database columns or Fillout form questions.
</Info>

## Troubleshooting

If your email did not arrive, click `Workflows` on top followed by your **email workflow**. Then, go to `History`.

/>

If you notice a red <Icon icon="circle-xmark" /> on your email workflow step, this means that there was an issue with the step completing. To fix this, ask Zite in the chat to check the workflow logs and attempt to fix the problem.
