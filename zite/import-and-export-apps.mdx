## Import Zite app

From the dashboard, click `App` followed by `Import existing Zite`.

![Screenshot 2026 03 13 At 3 41 33 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-13at3.41.33PM.png)

In the popup, drag and drop an existing **Zite JSON file**.

## Export Zite app

Hover over an app and click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> followed by `Export app`. A JSON file will automatically be downloaded by your device.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-13at3.51.31PM.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026 03 13 At 3 51 31 PM"
  title="Screenshot 2026 03 13 At 3 51 31 PM"
  style={{ width:"58%" }}
/>

\
You can toggle `Include all messages and history` in the popup if necessary. However, this will result in a larger file size.

## Move apps, forms, and databases

If you simply need to organize your apps, or forms and databases, you can drag and drop them to the appropriate workspace.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-13at3.51.58PM.png"
  alt="Screenshot 2026 03 13 At 3 51 58 PM"
  title="Screenshot 2026 03 13 At 3 51 58 PM"
  style={{ width:"68%" }}
/>

Alternatively, you can click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> followed by `Move to`. Then, click your chosen `folder`.
