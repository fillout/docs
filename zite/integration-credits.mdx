## Overview

Integration credits are separate from [AI credits](/zite/credits) and are consumed when your **published** apps interact with external services. Most integrated actions consume one credit, while some more resource-intensive actions may consume additional credits (see [table below](#what-costs-integration-credits)).

Each plan includes a monthly integration credit allowance that resets on the 1st of each month. You can view your usage on the [Billing & Usage](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing) page in your account settings. [Learn more](#check-your-usage).

<Check>
  **Zite Database operations are always free** and do not use any integration credits, regardless of your plan.
</Check>

## What costs integration credits

Integration credits are used any time your published app triggers an action that sends data to or retrieves data from an external service. Common examples include:

- **Uploading or downloading a file** via Google Drive (1 credit)
- **Triggering an action** in Twilio, Slack, or another connected service (1 credit)
- **Sending an email** to a user or team member (5 credits)
- **Generating a document** like a PDF (coming soon)

| Action | Credits per use |
| --- | --- |
| Standard integration (e.g. Twilio, Slack, OpenAI, Google Drive) | 1 |
| Email send | 5 |
| Document generation (coming soon) | 10 |
| Zite Database operations | Free (always) |

<Check>
  Database integrations like Airtable, Zite Database, and Google Sheets are free and do not consume integration credits.
</Check>

## Monthly limits by plan

Each plan includes a monthly integration credit allowance.

| Plan | Credits / month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 1,000 |
| Pro | 10,000 |
| Business | 50,000 |
| Team | 500,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom |

## How credits reset

Integration credits reset on the **1st of each month** (UTC). Unused credits do not roll over — each month starts fresh based on your plan.

## Check your usage

You can view your integration credit usage on the [Billing & Usage](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing) page in your account settings. The **Integration Credits** section shows a breakdown of your usage by type, along with how many credits you have remaining.

## FAQ

  <Accordion title="What if I need more integration credits?">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I need more integration credits than the Team plan includes?">

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Integration credits vs AI credits

| | AI credits | Integration credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Used for** | Building and modifying apps (chat, plan, and build modes) and AI features in Zite Databases | Published app interactions with external services |
| **When consumed** | While you're editing your app | When end users trigger workflows at runtime |

For more on how AI credits work, see the [AI Credits](/zite/credits) guide.
