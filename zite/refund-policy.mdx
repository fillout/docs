Learn how refunds and plan changes work for Zite.

## How it works

**New customers**

If you cancel your plan within the **first month** of a monthly or annual subscription, you may request a **full refund**, provided your account usage was minimal.

**Existing customers**

<Warning>
  If your upgrade after the first month was unintended, please downgrade your plan.
</Warning>

Refund requests made **within 14 days** of the upgrade charge are eligible for a full refund, provided usage has not exceeded the usage limit of the former plan. Any refund requests made after 14 days will be applied as **account credit** toward future invoices.

**Final charges**

Outside the situations described above, or in cases of significant usage, all charges are final. Refunds are **not available** for downtime, temporary issues, or performance-related concerns.

## How plan changes affect billing

You can [change or cancel your plan](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing) at any time. When you do:

- Your plan will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle, regardless of whether you have a monthly or annual plan.
- You will not be charged again unless you choose to upgrade or reactivate your plan.
- To remove your payment method, contact Zite support at [support@fillout.com](mailto:support@fillout.com).

Please review your billing and renewal settings regularly to avoid unexpected charges. Your billing settings are available in your [Billing and usage page](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing).

## How auto-upgrade works

If you add a credit card during your Fillout forms trial, your account will automatically upgrade at the end of your trial unless you opt out.

You can opt out of the auto-upgrade at any time before the end of the trial, and you will not be charged. Visit the [<u>Billing and Usage</u>](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing) page to **Downgrade to the Free plan**.

Once the trial ends, the account will continue with the option you selected.

## Zite AI credit usage and generative AI

Zite AI credits are consumed when generative AI features are used. Because generative AI is inherently flexible and exploratory, results can vary based on inputs, timing, and iteration.

This means it may not always be possible to achieve a specific outcome or consume the number of AI credits you originally anticipated. We recommend using the [Plan mode](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/plan-mode) as part of the creative process.

For this reason, **used AI credits cannot be refunded**, even if the results aren’t what you anticipated.

## Refunds related to disruptions and features

In some cases:

- An app within Zite may experience temporary issues.
- A specific feature you need may not be available.

While Zite works to improve the platform and resolve issues quickly for apps managed by Zite, these types of limitations are typically non-refundable.
