## Overview

Make changes to text and images generated on your app, which includes headers, descriptions, buttons, banners, section backgrounds, dividers, hyperlinks, icons, and input fields.

## Style elements in your app

Click `Edit` in the upper right corner followed by the element you'd like to customize.

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 03 23 At 4 23 29 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-23at4.23.29PM.png)
</Frame>

**Type a prompt** or **use the manual editor** to adjust font size, font weight, text color, background color, spacing, corner rounding, and shadows. Click `Save All` (above the chat box) to finalize edits.

<Note>
  Non-dynamic text can be edited directly in the app, while dynamic text requires a chat prompt.
</Note>

### When edits use credits

Some edits are free like changing text directly or making other quick tweaks. Once you add a prompt for an edit, it’s no longer free.

For full details on how credits work, check out our [credit usage guide](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/credits#credit-usage).

## Undo changes

Simply click `Undo` on the change you'd like to revert. Once undone, this action cannot be reversed.
