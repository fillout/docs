## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

Directly access your subscription details [here](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing).  `Browse plans` for your **Apps**, then choose your preferred plan in the popup by selecting `Get started` on the plan.

![Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 6 45 13 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at6.45.13PM.png)

<Warning>
  Only admins can modify a subscription plan.
</Warning>

If you're already on a paid plan, click `Modify`.

<img
  src="/images/modify.png"
  alt="Modify"
  title="Modify"
  style={{ width:"61%" }}
/>

Alternatively, go to your dashboard. Click your `Account name` on the upper left corner followed by `Settings`. Then, click `Billing & usage` in left side.

## Edit monthly credits

You can increase or decrease the monthly credits set for your account. On the **Billing & usage** settings, click `Browse plans` or `Modify` your Zite plan. \
Choose the number of `credits` from the dropdown, then `Increase or decrease credits`.

![Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 7 10 23 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at7.10.23PM.png)

## Cancel plan

You can [cancel your Zite app plan](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/cancel-account), and still keep your Fillout Forms plan. To cancel your Zite plan, **downgrade to the Free App plan**.

## The Team plan

If you are a frequent user of Fillout forms, Zite apps, and databases, we recommend checking out our [Team plan](https://www.fillout.com/help/team-plan).
