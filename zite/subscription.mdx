## Upgrade or downgrade your plan

Directly access your subscription details [here](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing). `Browse plans` for your **Apps** and choose your preferred plan.

![Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 6 45 13 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at6.45.13PM.png)

<Warning>
  Only admins can modify a subscription plan.
</Warning>

Alternatively, go to your dashboard. Click your `Account name` on the upper left corner followed by `Settings`. Then, click `Billing & usage` in left side.

## Change payment terms

To switch between monthly and annual billing, go to [Billing settings](https://build.fillout.com/home/settings/billing). Click `Browse plans` for the subscription you want to update (Forms or Apps), then toggle `Billed annually` on or off.

![Screenshot 2026 03 31 At 6 41 45 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-31at6.41.45PM.png)

## Change billing date

Billing dates can’t be directly edited. To change it, you’ll need to reset your subscription timing.

[Downgrade](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/subscription#upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan) your plan, then upgrade again on your preferred billing date. For example, if your current billing date is the 15th but you want it on the 1st, downgrade your plan before the 15th. Then upgrade again on the 1st. Your new billing date will now be every 1st of the month.

## Cancel plan

You can [cancel your Zite app plan](https://www.fillout.com/help/zite/cancel-account), and still keep your Fillout Forms plan. To cancel your Zite plan, **downgrade to the Free App plan**.

## The Team plan

If you are a frequent user of Fillout forms, Zite apps, and databases, we recommend checking out our [Team plan](https://www.fillout.com/help/team-plan).
