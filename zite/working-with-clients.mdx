## Set up an organization for each client

We recommend creating a separate [organization](/organizations) for each client. Each organization has its own apps, forms, databases, and billing plan, keeping client work fully isolated.

On the upper left corner, click your `Account name` followed by `Create organization`.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-10-09at4.28.45PM.png"
  alt="Create organization"
  title="Create organization"
  style={{ width:"41%" }}
/>

See [Organizations](/organizations) for more details on managing and switching between organizations.

## Add clients and team members

Invite clients or teammates to the appropriate organization. You can choose to invite them as:

- **Admin** — full access to all workspaces and settings within the client's organization
- **Member** — can create their own forms and apps, but cannot edit others'

On your dashboard, click `Invite members` in the upper right area.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2025-10-09at4.47.13PM.png"
  alt="Invite members"
  title="Invite members"

/>

## Export and deliver apps to clients

<Tip>
  Whenever possible, we recommend building apps, forms, and databases directly in the client's organization after you've [created it](/organizations). This keeps everything in one place from the start and avoids extra steps.
</Tip>

If you've already built something in a different organization — for example, a prototype or reusable template — you can transfer it between accounts.

Hover over an app and click <Icon icon="ellipsis" /> followed by `Export app`.

<img
  src="/images/Screenshot2026-03-13at3.51.31PM.png"
  alt="Export app"
  title="Export app"
  style={{ width:"58%" }}
/>

Then, switch to your client's organization and import the app from the dashboard by clicking `App` followed by `Import existing Zite`.

See [Import and export apps](/zite/import-and-export-apps) for the full guide.
